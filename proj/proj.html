What an Upstream Sound-Event Tag Costs, and Which Part of It: Claim, Packaging, and Declared Confidence

When a sound-event detector writes its decision into a multimodal model's prompt, is the effect caused by the claim, the way it is packaged, or its declared confidence? We measure them separately — then follow the cost into a real cascade.

Seunghun Oh* · Jaehong Myung* · Unsang Park†Sogang University · * Equal contribution · † Corresponding author

Research question

One message, three different levers.

In a real audio or audio–visual pipeline, upstream detections are commonly passed to a second model as plain-language tags. A false tag may compete with what the recording actually contains. But a full prompt mixes a factual claim, the field carrying it, and the serialization used to present it.

01 · CLAIM

What the detector asserts

Claim: “The clip does not contain the sound of X.”

Insert a matching or conflicting claim while holding the recording and target question fixed.

02 · DECLARED CONFIDENCE

What the field implies

Priority: high
versus
Confidence: high

Change only the epistemic field name, not the claim wording or graded value.

03 · PACKAGING

How it is delivered

Bare sentence

Source / Claim / Confidence block

Measure a bundled format effect, without attributing it to a single isolated formatting feature.

Controlled protocol

From matched prompts to a real detector cascade.

The study examines six reader models across sound events and additional diagnostic attributes. Eligible cells are selected using a metadata-free perceptual gate and checked against swapped answer ordering.

Not every part of the message costs the same.

Claim first

The upstream assertion is the largest step.

In the controlled condition walk, adding the false assertion causes more movement than simply changing how confidence is named for most reported ladder cells.

2.1–3.9×

Confidence versus Priority

Across the ten headline cells, the Confidence field produces this multiple of the Priority field's high-to-low claim-aligned margin swing. This is not an accuracy multiplier.

−6.6 to −11.1 pp

Cost of bare forwarding

Accuracy change from forwarding real CLAP tags rather than omitting them in all four order-robust cascade cells. Audio Flamingo 3 is a separate sensitivity case.

Break-even

When detector quality changes the answer

A stronger upstream stage can cross a reader-specific forwarding threshold. The result is conditional on the detector, reader, dataset and message serialization.
