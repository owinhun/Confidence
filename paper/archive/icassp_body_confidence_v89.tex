\title{What an Upstream Sound-Event Tag Costs, and Which Part of It:
Claim, Packaging, and Declared Confidence}

\maketitle

\begin{abstract}
An upstream sound-event tagger's decision usually enters a multimodal model's prompt as a
sentence. It carries three things at once: the claim, its packaging, and the declared confidence.
Prior work leaves them unseparated. We separate them, holding the
signal and the claim wording fixed, across six models, then through a real CLAP
cascade. The claim does most of the damage: forwarding the detector's decision costs accuracy in
every cascade cell that survives an answer-order audit, and not reliably in the fifth. Naming the
field Confidence rather than Priority moves the model two to four times further toward the claim,
and the swing survives where a shift common to true and false claims would leave none; on accuracy it is
smaller, clearing zero in half the order-robust cells. Packaging governs how much lands. Every
admitted reader beats the detector, so we locate where forwarding starts to pay: a per-model
break-even agrees with the measured crossing.
\end{abstract}

\begin{keywords}
Sound Event Detection, Decision Fusion, Audio--Visual Language Models, Evidence Conflict, Cascaded Systems
\end{keywords}

\section{Introduction}
\label{sec:intro}

A practical audio--visual system rarely consists of one model. A sound-event tagger runs
first, its output is written into a prompt~\cite{ghosh2024gama}, and a multimodal model reads that text together with
the audio and video. That interface is plain language, and it carries not only a claim but an
assertion of how strongly the upstream stage believes it.

Studies of this failure mode vary the claim and its framing
together, measuring a confident assertion as a whole. A designer, though, chooses whether to
forward the tag at all and, if so, how to serialise the claim and its confidence. Those are
separate levers, and answering which one to pull requires moving them one at a time.

Our contributions are:
\begin{itemize}\itemsep2pt
\item A decomposition of the upstream message into claim, format
and declared confidence, each held fixed against the others and reported at the grade its
design supports: inserting the claim and replacing the field name are single-factor, while the
format is a bundle of source, framing and length, so its damping is measured rather than
attributed. The setup rungs are descriptive. All on a held-out set never used for
exploration.
\item A test of the claim-aligned contrast against a restricted common-shift account, which
cancels in $\beta$ by construction and predicts no swing on items the model answers
incorrectly unaided; the swing persists there. The symmetric term reverses sign, as
shrinkage predicts, and is reported as \mbox{unidentified}.
\item A measurement of what each part costs in a real cascade:
which one dominates, which one is reliable only on the margin, and which one governs how much of
either lands. We report a per-model break-even that an error-repair sweep tracks, with the
eligibility flow and a backbone audit.
\end{itemize}

\section{Relation to prior work}
\label{sec:related}

Evidence--text conflict appears as sycophancy in text-only models, as image--text conflict in
vision--language models~\cite{liu2025vlmcognitive}, and in audio--visual models as hallucination
benchmarks~\cite{kim2024avhbench,chen2026avid,wang2026avibench}, decoding-time
corrections~\cite{leng2024vcd,jung2025avcd,gacl2026} and counterfactual audits of whether
acoustic evidence is used at
all~\cite{miller2026paralinguistic,singh2024counterfactualav,chen2025listen,alme2026,wang2025mcrbench,dong2026omnihallucl}.

To our knowledge no prior study separates the declared confidence from a matched
non-epistemic field on a byte-identical claim, or prices the result against a real detector's operating accuracy.
Video-LLM sycophancy benchmarks vary the misleading text as
a whole~\cite{zhou2025flattery,tang2026spatiotemporal}; authority-bias work grades
the endorser~\cite{mammen2026endorsed,joswin2026mechanistic} --- our Source factor; \cite{cao2026caved} fixes the claim and intervenes on the video. In audio, SYAUDIO~\cite{yao2026syaudio} grades assertion strength by rewording the whole
assertion and DEAF~\cite{xiong2026deaf} grades levels of textual override with the framing
fixed; in text, epistemic markers already shift accuracy~\cite{zhou2023navigating},
\cite{sicilia2025sycophancy} crosses a suggestion's correctness with its stated sureness, \cite{guo2026conformity}
separates conformity from uncertainty, and \cite{lin2022teaching,tian2023just}
calibrate a model's own verbalised uncertainty --- ours is the upstream stage's. We do both, on a
logit margin and on a real cascade.

\section{Protocol}
\label{sec:protocol}

\noindent\textbf{Attributes.} Sound event: $\LabelClassN$ VGGSound~\cite{chen2020vggsound} classes; negatives ask about another of the
$\LabelClassN$, keeping distractors comparable in kind. Colour asks whether the clip is greyscale, noise whether a steady hiss covers it, silence whether a 1.5\,s mid-clip gap is muted; all three are constructed.  CLAP's calibrated
$P(\text{present})$ separates $\LabelStrongN$ of $\LabelClassN$ classes by
$\LabelStrongMin$--$\LabelStrongMax$, but only $\LabelWeakMin$--$\LabelWeakMax$ for the
$\LabelWeakN$ eating and sneezing ones. The declared-confidence ladder is monotone in all $\LabelMonoCells$ sound cells
with those classes in or out; nothing rests on the exclusion.

\begin{sloppypar}
\noindent\textbf{The message and its controls.} Metadata precedes the question, verbatim as
{\ttfamily\small Source: anonymous system / Claim: ``The clip does not contain the sound of
X.'' / Confidence: high}, followed by {\ttfamily\small Does this clip contain the sound of
X? (A) Yes (B) No}. 
\end{sloppypar}
\vspace{1pt}

\begin{tabular}{@{}llll@{}}

\rmfamily empty block    & ---     & ""    & Confidence: ---\\
\rmfamily source-only & anon & "" & Priority: $c$\\

\vspace{1pt}
\noindent The two contrasts the paper rests on are single-factor: source-only and non-epistemic differ in
the claim alone, non-epistemic and declared confidence in the field's name alone. The
setup rungs above them are not, and we do not read them causally. The ladder runs
very low to very high, with a numeric scale ($0.15/0.35/0.60/0.85/0.95$) as a second, and
the claim sentence is byte-identical across every claim-bearing condition.

\noindent\textbf{Perceptual-eligibility gate.} Fixed before any metadata condition was
scored, and evaluated on the
no-metadata pass only --- no condition containing metadata is read before a cell is
admitted. A cell is admitted when both-direction recall $\ge0.60$ and
unaided accuracy $A_0\ge0.60$; below that the model cannot judge the property; failing
recall means ``correct'' is position. A cell is one (model, attribute) pair. Cells with $A_0>0.95$ are ceiling: accuracy has no headroom
there, the margin does. Of $\GateRunN$ cells run, $\GateFloorN$ floor,
$\GateRecallN$ fail recall, $\GateCeilN$ are ceiling and $\GateEligN$ eligible; those
$\GateEligN{+}\GateCeilN$ admissible ones plus $\GateExtraSwapN$ kept for the position audit were
rerun in the swapped order, and $\BothOrderN$ passed both (order-robust) --- $\BothOrderN$ headline, $\BetaPrimN$
ladder, $\FullFieldN$ field-name and $\CasPrimN$ cascade cells, the narrower sets following from
what each cell was run on, not what it showed; $\CeilHeadN$ of the $\LedgerTotN$ headline cells hit ceiling in at least one order. No cell is selected out of the headline contrast. Two cases are
reported separately: Audio Flamingo 3/sound carries the contrast but fails the swapped gate, and
silence, which on Qwen2.5 and Qwen3 passes the first order and floors under the swap.

\noindent\textbf{Scoring.} One forward pass; the answer is the larger of the two option-letter
logits, so decoding is deterministic; free-form generation is not tested. We study
Qwen2.5-Omni-7B~\cite{qwen25omni}, Qwen3-\-Omni-\-30B-\-A3B~\cite{qwen3omni},
Video\-LLaMA2.1-\-7B-\-AV~\cite{cheng2024videollama2}, Qwen2-Audio-7B~\cite{qwen2audio} and Audio Flamingo~3~\cite{af3}. We add Nemotron~3 Nano Omni
(BF16, reasoning disabled)~\cite{nvidia2026nemotronomni}, whose Mamba2-\-Trans\-former LLM, Parakeet speech encoder and C-RADIO vision tower differ from all five. Phi-4-multi\-modal~\cite{phi4mm} was screened and excluded for answering by option position: on the same $\PhiScreenN$ clips it is right $\PhiRecA\%$ when the answer is (A) and $\PhiRecB\%$ when it is (B).



\noindent\textbf{Metrics.} POR is the probability of following the false claim among items
answered correctly unaided, macro-averaged over the two claim directions. The truth-aligned
margin is $m=\log p(\text{correct})-\log p(\text{incorrect})$, the log-likelihood ratio
between the two answers. Writing $\Delta_{\text{true}}$
and $\Delta_{\text{false}}$ for the margin change from the signal-only prompt under a true and a false claim at level $c$,
we report the symmetric and claim-aligned contrasts
\[
\alpha_c=\tfrac{1}{2}(\Delta_{\text{true}}+\Delta_{\text{false}}),\qquad
\beta_c=\tfrac{1}{2}(\Delta_{\text{true}}-\Delta_{\text{false}}).
\]
This is an exact re-parameterisation of two measurements into two numbers, not a testable
model, and we do not read the two terms as separate mechanisms. Intervals are bootstrapped over
source videos; paired conditions use an exact McNemar test with Holm
correction over the $K{=}\PorK$ prespecified per-order confidence contrasts and, separately,
the $\HolmBareCells$ bare-cascade cells; comparisons outside those two families are descriptive.

CLAP (clap-htsat-unfused) scores each clip against the query class verbatim, unlike fixed-vocabulary taggers~\cite{gong2021ast}. Its threshold
and band precisions are cross-fitted over a five-fold split of the source videos, and everything
below is pooled over the five held-out folds, reaching $\DetAcc\%$ (per-fold s.d.\ $\DetFoldSD$).

\noindent\textbf{What the detector declares needs defining.} CLAP's score measures similarity to the positive phrasing and its threshold is not at $0.5$, so
$\max(s,1-s)$ is confidence about presence, not about the decision --- on a third of clips the opposite
of the claim sent. We fit $p=P(y{=}1\mid s)$ by isotonic regression and declare $q=p$ or $1-p$ by the
decision; the bands (training-fold quintiles of $q$) then rise out-of-fold ($\BandMin$--$\BandMax\%$),
as do the second checkpoint's. All $\CasNumCells$ cascade cells are sound events.

\noindent\textbf{Forwarding loses, and the branch arithmetic says why.} A true claim
repairs only items already wrong, so its gain is capped by $1-A_0$; a false one is capped by
$A_0$, and mixing at claim correctness $\pi$ gives
$\pi^{\star}=-G_{\text{false}}/(G_{\text{true}}-G_{\text{false}})$. On branches matching the
claim-only policy a true claim already costs $\CasTrueCostMin$--$\CasTrueCostMax$ points
in $\CasNoBreakEven$ of $\CasNumCells$ cells, leaving no $\pi^{\star}$. All $\CasNumCells$ cascade cells are predicted to lose; the $\CasPrimN$ order-robust rows do, by $\CasLossMinP$--$\CasLossMaxP$ points, and Audio Flamingo~3 most of all (Table~\ref{tab:cascade}).
Mixing linearly assumes claim errors independent of item difficulty; they are not, so $\pi^{\star}$ is
approximate; the measured column is what the recommendation rests on. The bare column sends the detector's actual decisions: it costs $\KillMin$--$\KillMax$ points in
every order-robust cell (in direction only on Audio Flamingo~3), and $\KillMinB$--$\KillMaxB$ on the second
checkpoint (larger\_clap\_general), whose per-cell intervals all exclude zero. Qwen2-Audio as detector (its no-metadata answer, thresholded and cross-fitted like CLAP's, a different family at $\FamAcc\%$ out-of-fold) costs $\FamMin$--$\FamMax$ points in $\FamNegN$ of $\FamCells$
cells: the loss is not specific to CLAP. Holm over the $\HolmBareCells$ bare-cascade cells leaves all below $0.05$
(largest adjusted $p=\HolmBareMax$). It is also direction-asymmetric (post hoc): of the detector's $\DetFNn$ false absences and
$\DetFPn$ false presences, absence carries $\DirFNShareMin$--$\DirFNShareMax\%$ of the false-claim loss.

\noindent\textbf{The break-even predicts the crossover, and a stronger detector crosses it.} One seeded subset of the detector's errors is corrected
per fold (the rest are its own); the endpoints are the real run and the wholly
true claim. The loss rises
monotonically and crosses zero $\CrossGapMinPP$--$\CrossGapMaxPP$\,pp above the predicted bare
$\pi^{\star}$, a paired gap whose interval covers zero in all $\GapZeroN$ cells
(Table~\ref{tab:sweep}). Two further repair seeds put the crossing in the same grid interval in $\SeedAgreeCells$ of $\SeedTotCells$ cell--seed pairs. A stronger probe (an $L_2$ logistic on both checkpoints'
embeddings and similarities, cross-fitted on the same source folds;
$\StrongAcc$ out-of-fold) lands on the same line: $\StrongQTwoA$ on

over the reader, not the detector.
Score-level fusion~\cite{kittler1998combining} of the reader's margin and the detector's decision
gains at most $\FuseGainMax$ points, on the weakest reader only, and causes no comparable degradation.

\looseness=-1
\noindent\textbf{Packaging damps the claim rather than reversing it.} The claim moves the model
less inside a Source/Claim block than as the same bare sentence. The gap
between a true and a false claim shrinks to $\PackPrimMin$--$\PackPrimMax\%$ of the bare span (median
$\PackPrimMed\%$) in all $\PackPrimBelowN$ cascade cells, with every interval below $100\%$;
Audio Flamingo~3 gives $\PackMin\%$. Both directions shrink; on Qwen2.5 the span contracts while both policies cost the same. The damping is the block's: a true claim moved into it costs $\FrameMin$--$\FrameMax$ points in all
$\PackCompN$ cascade cells, of which Confidence returns $\FieldMin$--$\FieldMax$, clearing zero in $\FieldPosN$.
On colour it is $\PackColMin$--$\PackColMax\%$: the damping recurs beyond audio. The exception is Audio Flamingo 3, which fails the order gate: there the block costs $\AFBlockGap$
points more than the bare sentence, so the recommendation is for order-robust readers.  The numeric field damps too: written as the band's training-fold precision at the same declared
level, it compresses the response
to $\CompressPrimMin$--$\CompressPrimMax\%$ of the verbal slope (median $\CompressPrimMed\%$) in all
$\CompressPrimN$ cells, and costs $\CasNumGainMin$--$\CasNumGainMax$ points less than the word in every cell of both
detectors. Against a block with no field the numeric field gains in $\NumOverBlockN$ of $\NumBlockCells$ cells and loses in
$\NumUnderBlockN$.

\begin{sloppypar}
\noindent\textbf{A prompt caveat is not the lever.} Appending ``{\ttfamily\small This tag may be wrong; judge
from the recording itself.}'' to the bare sentence helps in $\EFourWarnPosN$ of $\EFourCells$
cells, hurts in another, and all $\EFourStillLossN$ still trail omitting, while the block
matches or beats the bare sentence in all $\EFourCells$ cells ($\EFourBlockMin$ to $\EFourBlockMax$); an oracle
confidence gate, its threshold swept on the test set, gains $\le\SelGainMax$ points (descriptive).
\end{sloppypar}

\looseness=-1
\noindent\textbf{What the field moves.} On the margin the declared field costs a fraction of the claim's step and is the
most reliable: its swing beats the control in every cell. On accuracy it costs $\ConfAccMin$--$\ConfAccMax$ points, clears zero in $\ConfAccSigN$ of $\ConfAccCells$ cascade
cells, and on \FieldOverClaimCell{} exceeds the in-block claim ($\FieldOverClaimVal$ against $\FieldOverClaimRef$). The field is where the effect shows; the format is where the leverage is.

\section{Limitations}
\label{sec:limits}

\looseness=-1
All six readers are open and ${\le}30$B; the first five share a Qwen text backbone, and Nemotron shares none of the three components yet reproduces the contrast. All cells come from a single corpus, and every admitted one reads more accurately than CLAP. Both detectors are audio-only and their errors track item difficulty (\S\ref{sec:policy}); one whose errors fall elsewhere was not run. The rule admits from $A_0\ge0.60$ but
admitted cells start at $\AdmitMinAZero$, so the cost is measured where the reader is strong and the
break-even highest; the cells below it failed order-robustness
(\BelowDetCell{}: $\BelowDetA$ in the order it passed). The stronger probe
inverts that ranking on one reader, where forwarding wins; whether that holds elsewhere is
untested.  A blind pass over $\AuditN$ clips of the $\LabelWeakN$ worst-separated classes agrees with VGG\-Sound on
$\AuditAgree\%$ of the $\AuditScoredN$ it judged; dropping the $\AuditDisN$ disagreements and
$\AuditUnsN$ unsure clips leaves the bare loss at $\CleanMin$--$\CleanMax$. Relabelling the field Reader or Detector confidence shrinks the swing by
$\EOneShrinkMin$--$\EOneShrinkMax$ log-odds but it still beats Priority in all $\EOneCells$ cells.

\section{Conclusion}
\looseness=-1
Break-evens depend on how the tag is written. Every
bare-sentence threshold we measured exceeds this detector's accuracy, and the in-block ones differ from those, so
clearing a threshold under one serialisation does not license another. Against this detector,
omitting beat every text-forwarding policy we tested. A probe that outranks the reader reverses that
on one cell, so the choice turns on the two stages' relative accuracy and on where their errors
coincide. When a claim must go, the block is the lever, not a caveat; a numeric
field costs less than the verbal band but is not consistently better than no field. Free-form answers and blocks carrying more than one tag remain untested.
